### 1.4 Third-Party Material
**Third-Party Material** means material for which the Licensor does not have authority to grant the permissions in this Licence. Third-Party Material remains governed by its own terms.

### 1.5 Covered Improvement
A **Covered Improvement** means a modification, adaptation, addition or other work that, to the extent recognised by applicable law, is based on, changes, replaces, extends, translates, adapts or incorporates protected expression from the Work.

A Covered Improvement does **not**, merely because it interoperates with the Work, include:

1. an independently created application, service or component;
2. a general-purpose dependency used without modification;
3. user, customer, beneficiary or donor data;
4. a database populated independently of the Work;
5. confidential configuration or credentials;
6. an independent work communicating with the Work through an ordinary interface or protocol; or
7. independently created material that does not incorporate protected expression from the Work.

This definition does not enlarge copyright, patent or other exclusive rights beyond applicable law.

### 1.6 Preferred Editable Form
**Preferred Editable Form** means the form actually preferred for making modifications to the relevant material, including source code for software, editable/native design files for designs, and editable source documents for written works.

### 1.7 Legal Entity and Control
A **Legal Entity** includes an organisation exercising rights under QGB together with entities it **Controls**, that Control it, or that are under common Control with it where aggregation is required by this Licence.

**Control** means direct or indirect power to direct management or material policies, whether by ownership, voting rights, contract or another arrangement.

### 1.8 QGB Revenue
**QGB Revenue** means gross revenue, before deduction of ordinary costs and expenses, reasonably attributable to a product, service or commercial activity in which the Work or a Covered Improvement is a material functional, creative, commercial or customer-acquisition component.

QGB Revenue excludes:

1. sales, value-added, goods-and-services or similar taxes collected for government;
2. bona fide refunds and chargebacks; and
3. internal transfers between Controlled Entities.

Where the Work is genuinely incidental to a larger offering, only the portion reasonably attributable to the Work or Covered Improvement is QGB Revenue. The Licensee must use a reasonable, documented and consistently applied allocation method.

A transaction, corporate structure, licence, transfer, bundle, zero-price arrangement or related-party arrangement must not be structured principally to avoid QGB Revenue or an obligation under QGB.

### 1.9 Cumulative QGB Revenue
**Cumulative QGB Revenue** means QGB Revenue accumulated by the Licensee and relevant Controlled Entities from the applicable Work and Covered Improvements from the date commercial exploitation under the applicable QGB Licence begins, without resetting because of a change of financial year, corporate reorganisation, internal transfer or change in accounting policy.

### 1.10 Public Record
A **Public Record** required by QGB must:

1. be accessible to the public without payment, account creation or NDA;
2. remain publicly available for at least five years;
3. identify the Licensee and the applicable Work;
4. identify the relevant reporting period and QGB obligation;
5. identify recipients and amounts or values where a Give-Back is required; and
6. contain evidence reasonably sufficient for an independent member of the public to understand and verify substantial compliance without requiring disclosure prohibited by Section 5.

A public repository, ordinary public website or comparable durable public location may satisfy this requirement.

### 1.11 Qualifying Give-Back
A **Qualifying Give-Back** means an irrevocable contribution of money, goods, professional services, infrastructure, access, resources or other objectively measurable value directed to genuine:

- charitable activity;
- community benefit;
- independent creator development;
- education or research for public benefit;
- accessibility;
- environmental benefit;
- humanitarian or social-purpose activity; or
- another substantially similar public-benefit purpose.

A Qualifying Give-Back must provide substantial benefit beyond the Licensee and its related parties.

The following do **not** qualify merely by being described as giving back:

1. ordinary wages or remuneration owed by the Licensee;
2. ordinary supplier payments;
3. advertising, sponsorship or marketing whose principal purpose is promotion of the Licensee;
4. customer acquisition expenditure;
5. discounts, refunds or customer credits;
6. taxes, fines, penalties or legally compulsory payments;
7. purchases made principally for the Licensee's own benefit;
8. circular transactions;
9. transfers to the Licensee or its Controlled Entities;
10. transfers to the Licensor merely because that person is the Licensor;
11. transfers to founders, directors, executives or close related parties of the Licensee or Licensor; or
12. a contribution conditioned on the recipient surrendering unrelated intellectual property or providing disproportionate commercial benefit to the Licensee.

### 1.12 Qualifying Public-Benefit Organisation
A **Qualifying Public-Benefit Organisation** means an organisation currently registered or formally recognised as a charity, not-for-profit or equivalent public-benefit body by an applicable governmental regulator or statutory register in its home jurisdiction, and not established principally for private profit or private owner benefit.

### 1.13 Give-Back Record
A **Give-Back Record** is a Public Record describing a required Qualifying Give-Back, including recipient, date, amount or objectively reasonable value, nature of the contribution, applicable reporting period and evidence reasonably sufficient to verify the contribution.

### 1.14 Impact Record
An **Impact Record** is a Public Record describing, in reasonable non-sensitive terms:

1. what the Work was used for;
2. the charitable, community or public purpose served;
3. material outcomes, lessons or limitations; and
4. specific transferable learning that another public-benefit organisation could use.

An Impact Record does not require publication of the private integration itself.

## 4. No Central Beneficiary and No QGB Toll

QGB is a licensing framework, not a beneficiary of the Give-Back obligation.

Unless a separate voluntary agreement expressly provides otherwise:

1. no Qualifying Give-Back is payable to a person merely because they created QGB;
2. no Qualifying Give-Back is payable to the Licensor merely because they licensed the Work;
3. no central QGB or Quant organisation is entitled to receive Give-Back funds; and
4. satisfaction of a Give-Back obligation must benefit qualifying independent people, communities or public-purpose activity.

The purpose is not to make success flow backwards. It is to make opportunity flow forwards.

# Part II — QGB-1.0

## Build. Grow. Give Back.

QGB-1.0 is the success-triggered member of the QGB family.

It is intended to let a person start small, learn, modify, commercialise and grow. As substantial economic value accumulates, reciprocity grows with it.

## 16. QGB-1.0 Grant

Subject to QGB-1.0, the Licensor grants You a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free copyright licence, for the duration of applicable copyright, to:

1. use and study the Work;
2. reproduce the Work;
3. modify and adapt the Work;
4. create Covered Improvements;
5. publicly perform or display the Work where the relevant rights apply;
6. distribute copies of the Work and Covered Improvements; and
7. commercially exploit the Work and Covered Improvements.

You may charge money and build a business. Success is not itself a breach.

The graduation obligations below are conditions of continued exercise of this grant after the relevant threshold is reached.

---

## 17. QGB-1 Graduation — US$1,000,000

When Cumulative QGB Revenue first reaches **US$1,000,000**, knowledge gives back.

Within **90 days** after the threshold is reached, You must publicly provide:

1. the Preferred Editable Form of Covered Improvements materially used in generating the QGB Revenue;
2. reasonable information necessary to understand, build or use those Covered Improvements where applicable; and
3. the attribution and QGB licence notices required by this Licence.

You are **not** required to publish unrelated proprietary systems, independent interoperating components, private datasets, customer information, credentials, security-sensitive information, or unrelated trade secrets.

Covered Improvements published to satisfy this Section must be licensed under **QGB-1.0**, unless the original Licensor has expressly identified another licence as a **QGB-Compatible Reciprocal Licence** for that Work.

Once properly published, later QGB Revenue does not require republication of unchanged material. Material new Covered Improvements used in the QGB activity must be added to the public release within 90 days after first material commercial use following QGB-1 graduation.

---

## 18. QGB-2 Graduation — US$2,000,000

When Cumulative QGB Revenue first reaches **US$2,000,000**, You must continue complying with Section 17 and, within **45 days**, publish a **Give-Back Commitment**.

The Give-Back Commitment must identify:

1. at least one charitable, community, independent-creator, educational or other Qualifying Give-Back purpose You commit to support;
2. the form of support;
3. how frequently support will occur; and
4. how the public will be able to verify performance.

Within **90 days** after QGB-2 graduation, You must publish the first Give-Back Record showing genuine performance of that commitment.

Thereafter, while You continue commercially exploiting the Work or Covered Improvements and remain above this graduation point, You must publish at least one Give-Back Record for each 12-month period.

The QGB-2 Graduation commitment must be genuine, continuing, measurable and not merely promotional. The applicable obligations are those stated in this Licence; no additional payment to the Licensor or to Quant is implied.

---

## 19. QGB-100 Graduation — US$100,000,000

When Cumulative QGB Revenue first reaches **US$100,000,000**, success gives forward at scale.

Beginning with the first full calendar week after the threshold is reached, You must make Qualifying Give-Back contributions totalling at least:

> **US$50,000 for every applicable calendar week.**

For clarity, 52 applicable weeks require at least **US$2,600,000** of Qualifying Give-Back.

The weekly obligation is a minimum for each week. You may distribute a week's amount among multiple qualifying recipients.

A contribution for a later week does not retrospectively satisfy an earlier missed week except as part of a cure under Section 12.

Each weekly contribution must appear in a Give-Back Record no later than **30 days after the end of the calendar month** containing that week.

The QGB-100 obligation continues for each week during which You continue commercial exploitation of the applicable Work or Covered Improvements after QGB-100 graduation. Corporate restructuring or transferring the QGB activity within the same Controlled group does not reset graduation.

---

## 20. Change of Control and Acquisition under QGB-1.0

If a Licensee that has not reached a QGB-1.0 graduation point is acquired by, merged into, or comes under Control of a Legal Entity whose aggregated QGB activity causes an applicable graduation condition to be satisfied, the applicable graduation obligation begins no later than **90 days after the change of Control**, unless it applied earlier.

This provision prevents a large organisation from acquiring a small QGB user solely to retain treatment intended for a growing independent user.

---

## 21. QGB-1.0 Keep-Growing Rule

Meeting a graduation threshold does not:

1. cap Your revenue;
2. transfer ownership of Your business to the Licensor;
3. transfer unrelated intellectual property to the Licensor;
4. require payment to the Licensor; or
5. prevent continued commercial growth.

The bargain is continued permission under QGB-1.0 in exchange for the reciprocity applicable at the scale You have reached.

**Build. Grow. Give Back. Keep Growing.**

# Part V — QGB Principles

These principles explain why the licences exist. They do not replace the operative provisions above.

### Build.
Someone gave you enough room to begin. Use it. Learn. Experiment. Make something useful. Make something strange. Make something better.

### Grow.
You are allowed to succeed. QGB is not a vow of poverty and it is not a mechanism for keeping independent creators small.

### Give Back.
If shared work contributes to meaningful success, leave something useful behind: knowledge, opportunity, resources, community benefit or public purpose, according to the QGB licence you accepted.

### Keep Growing.
Giving back is not the end of the bargain. It is part of growth.

**Knowledge gives back. Success gives forward.**

**Small acts should scale with success, not disappear because of it.**

**You were given a ladder. If you climb it, do not pull it up behind you.**

QGB does not ask that every good thing flow back to its origin.

It asks that opportunity keep moving.
