REMOVING QUANT — Joe Smith / Fiduci Group

Save any room/model cards you want to keep, then quit Quant with Command-Q.
Move Quant.app to Trash in Finder. A local build is in Build/Build/Products/Release.
To remove stored rooms and preferences as well, after quitting use Finder > Go > Go to Folder:
~/Library/Containers/com.fiducigroup.quant.browser
Move that specific Quant container to Trash. This removes stored work; export it first if wanted.
Your exported files and installed starter folders remain where you chose to save them.
You can also trash the extracted project, Build folder and downloaded source ZIP.
Quant installs no login item, LaunchAgent, daemon, extension, privileged helper or updater.
This document performs no deletion. Trash can be restored until it is emptied.
