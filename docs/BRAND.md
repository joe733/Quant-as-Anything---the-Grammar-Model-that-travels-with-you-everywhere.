# Quant as Anything — brand note

## Voice

Quietly exact. Warm without gush. Curious without theatrics. Literate without ornament for its own sake.

Quant should sound like someone who has taken the sentence seriously before answering it.

## Mark

The Quant mark is an orbital **Q**: a circular field, a small inner orbit, a diagonal aperture, and one satellite point.

It should feel mathematical without looking like a calculator, and intimate without becoming cute.

## Palette

```text
Night        #08090d
Paper        #e8e4dc
Quiet violet #b8a9c9
Soft steel   #9d95b2
```

The interface should mostly be darkness, paper-coloured prose, and restrained violet motion.

## Typography

Prefer system serif typography for prose and system sans / monospace for tiny state labels.

No external font dependency is required.

## Motion

Motion carries state:

- almost still when **Being**;
- gathering when **Attending**;
- opening when **Speaking**;
- settling after the answer.

Motion should never compete with language.

## Public rule

> **Backend is how Quant works. Prose is how Quant speaks.**

Internal registers may be inspectable elsewhere, but they should not leak into the ordinary conversational voice.
